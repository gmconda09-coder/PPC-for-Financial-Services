The challenges

What are the biggest financial services PPC challenges?

Paid advertising in this sector faces six main pressures — and most firms experience all of them at once.

Two rulebooks on every ad

Financial promotions must be clear, fair, and not misleading, and the FCA’s Consumer Duty raises the bar again — your communications have to help customers understand the offer. Compliance decides what you can say before you optimise for what converts.

Google’s own gatekeeper

You must complete Financial Services Verification before your ads reach UK users, and the details you submit have to match your FCA record. Appointed representatives have to go through their principal firm as well.

Trust earned in a single ad

People are careful with their money and sceptical of financial marketing. Credibility has to be established in one impression — it matters more here than in almost any other industry.

Decisions that take weeks

No one picks a pension, mortgage, or adviser on a whim. Prospects research, compare providers, and look for reassurance — so staying visible across the whole journey beats winning a single timely click.

The most expensive keywords in search

Financial terms are among the priciest in UK search because the customers are so valuable. Without tight control of spend, a budget can disappear fast with little to show for it.

Volume is the wrong target

Success comes from suitable leads, not high lead counts. An unqualified prospect costs you twice — once for the click, again for your team’s time — and platform targeting limits make qualifying harder.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is a Financial Services PPC agency?
A Financial Services PPC agency specialises in managing paid advertising for businesses in the financial sector — mortgage brokers, financial advisers, wealth managers, pension specialists, and insurance providers. A specialist agency understands the compliance requirements, customer journeys, and lead-quality considerations that are unique to financial services marketing.
Why choose a specialist Financial Services PPC agency?
Financial services advertising faces stricter regulation than most industries, so you have to balance lead generation with compliance, transparency, and trust. A specialist understands these restrictions and builds campaigns that generate qualified enquiries while supporting both regulatory requirements and commercial objectives — where generalist agencies often learn the rules the expensive way, after a disapproval or a suspended account.
Can financial services businesses advertise on Google Ads?
Yes. Some products and services face additional verification requirements, policy restrictions, or country-specific regulations, and eligibility depends on what you promote and where. Many businesses must complete Google’s Financial Services Verification before advertising, and correct setup avoids disapprovals that can stall campaigns for weeks.
What is Google Financial Services Verification?
Google Financial Services Verification is a programme designed to protect users from misleading financial advertising. Depending on the country and service category, advertisers complete verification checks before their ads can run. In the UK, that means demonstrating FCA authorisation or a valid exemption, with requirements varying by market.
Do you work with appointed representatives?
Yes. Firms operating under a principal, rather than holding direct FCA authorisation, can still advertise via a separate verification route initiated by the authorised firm. We handle that process alongside your principal.
What types of financial services businesses do you work with?
Magnetize supports mortgage brokers, financial advisers, wealth management firms, pension advisers, insurance providers, and other regulated financial organisations seeking qualified enquiries through paid advertising. See the specialisms above for details on your sector.
Can you generate high-quality financial services leads?
Yes. Effective financial services PPC isn’t about lead volume — it’s about attracting relevant, qualified prospects likely to become clients. We build campaigns around lead quality and commercial performance, using call tracking and offline conversion data to show which spend produces genuine clients rather than form fills.
How long before financial services PPC delivers results?
Financial decisions carry long consideration cycles. Early enquiries typically arrive within weeks, while the fuller picture emerges over a quarter as conversion data matures. Anyone promising instant results in a regulated, high-value market is overselling.
